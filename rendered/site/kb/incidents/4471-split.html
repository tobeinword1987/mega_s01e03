  1. Parser reads dates month first. Tenants that write the day first see day and month swapped. This is the bug the desk already knew about. It covers most of the reports.
  2. The booking-v3 form hint says MM/DD/YYYY. The form tells people to type the month first, and the tenant then reads it day first. The parser fix does not touch this: the hint text and the form need their own change. These reports all arrived through the form.

How the reports were sorted

  • One id, one report. The ticket system exports in overlapping pages, so the same report can come twice. The counts use each id once.
  • The tenant decides the cause, not intake. intakeTag is a guess made from the reporter’s browser. The tenant’s locale and booking form say which bug it was.
  • A report counts where it is filed. A few reports read as a one-day move, not a swap. They stay under the cause of their tenant, and the table footnotes them.
